# PUMP dynamic edit — 30-second test

**Source audio:** original narration and music, source time 01:34.14–02:04.14.  
**Frame:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps.  
**Rule:** no source-video frames, old cards, price values, forecast, or invented data. Chart axes are unlabeled. All illustrative photography is newly generated.

## Treatment

- **Promise:** A bonding curve reacts to demand and sales; it does not judge whether the project is valuable.
- **Identity:** High-contrast charcoal, acid yellow, cobalt, cyan, and coral; condensed heavy Russian type.
- **Hero:** A moving, unlabeled curve that changes from an incoming demand path to a sales path, then breaks into a question.
- **Motion:** Hard punch cuts, traced curves, orbiting tokens, directional camera pushes, kinetic words, and brief shakes only on the strongest beats.
- **Arc:** Demand → price direction → limits of the formula → growth is not proof → participant exit changes the picture.
- **Payoff:** The curve visibly reconfigures under “КАРТИНА МЕНЯЕТСЯ”; no end card.

## Verification targets

- New visuals only; original narration and music only.
