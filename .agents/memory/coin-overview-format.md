name: Coin overview format
description: Content, source, and visual rules for the third crypto Shorts format.
---

«Обзор монет» is a separate third crypto format; keep formats 1 and 2 unchanged. Its fixed six-scene arc is: coin/current price and seven-day change; seven-day price history and range; open interest or a clearly labelled 24-hour volume; BTC/ETH comparison; a retrospective $100 calculation; then risk and source attribution.

Keep the HYPE-inspired dark vertical graphic treatment with restrained mint and gold accents, a dedicated music bed, and a clear final risk/source card. Do not reuse HYPE’s token figures, logo, or claims for another coin. Append the approved seven-day exchange CTA after the risk/source narration; do not alter the factual scenes.

Use CoinGecko for coin matching, current price, seven-day history, market volume, and BTC/ETH comparison. Require an unambiguous coin match and a complete seven-day history. Open interest may be shown only when a matching Hyperliquid perpetual is available, and must be described as Hyperliquid-only, never as total market open interest. Otherwise use CoinGecko’s 24-hour volume and label it as volume. Show when the data were retrieved and name the relevant sources.

For coins selected from a ranked CoinGecko list, carry the selected CoinGecko ID through the saved job and resolve that ID directly for the overview; do not re-resolve the selection from its ticker alone. Manually entered topics still use the exact-match search path.

The $100 example is historical and must exclude neither fees, taxes, nor slippage from its disclaimer; it is not a forecast. Do not claim that a price move was caused by another metric. Close with volatility risk and state that the video is educational, not investment advice.

**Why:** HYPE’s story structure is reusable, but its values and token-specific claims are not. A single exchange’s open interest can be misleading if presented as market-wide. The user chose a fixed referral CTA, but it must not be mixed into factual narration or mistaken for a trade signal.

**Why:** A ranked-list selection identifies a specific asset, while tickers can be shared by unrelated coins. Preserving the ID prevents the generated overview from silently switching to a different asset.

**How to apply:** Save the chosen coin’s market snapshot, timestamp, and source labels with the plan before voice synthesis and rendering. Keep the CTA as a separate final voice segment after risk/source attribution. Keep scene order and visual theme isolated from the other crypto formats; if required data is missing or ambiguous, explain the failure rather than inventing a value.
