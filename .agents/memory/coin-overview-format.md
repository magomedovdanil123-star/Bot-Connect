«Обзор монет» is a separate third crypto format; keep formats 1 and 2 unchanged. Its fixed six-scene arc is: coin/current price and seven-day change; seven-day price history and range; open interest or a clearly labelled 24-hour volume; BTC/ETH comparison; a retrospective $100 calculation; then risk and source attribution.

Keep the HYPE-inspired dark vertical graphic treatment with restrained mint and gold accents, a dedicated music bed, and a clear final risk/source card. Do not reuse HYPE’s token figures, logo, or claims for another coin. The format uses a fixed data-driven script without a user CTA.

Use CoinGecko for coin matching, current price, seven-day history, market volume, and BTC/ETH comparison. Require an unambiguous coin match and a complete seven-day history. Open interest may be shown only when a matching Hyperliquid perpetual is available, and must be described as Hyperliquid-only, never as total market open interest. Otherwise use CoinGecko’s 24-hour volume and label it as volume. Show when the data were retrieved and name the relevant sources.

The $100 example is historical and must exclude neither fees, taxes, nor slippage from its disclaimer; it is not a forecast. Do not claim that a price move was caused by another metric. Close with volatility risk and state that the video is educational, not investment advice.

**Why:** HYPE’s story structure is reusable, but its values and token-specific claims are not. A single exchange’s open interest can be misleading if presented as market-wide, and persisted video jobs must not silently change facts after a resume.

**How to apply:** Save the chosen coin’s market snapshot, timestamp, and source labels with the plan before voice synthesis and rendering. Keep the scene order and visual theme isolated from the other crypto formats; if any required source data is missing or ambiguous, explain the failure rather than inventing a value.
