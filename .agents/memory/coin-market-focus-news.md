---
name: Financial news sourcing
description: Publisher-source and evidence rules for finance market-focus Shorts and long-form topic research.
---

Use direct publisher or official-project feeds for the headline. Require a recent, coin-relevant item whose HTTPS article URL stays on the publisher's domain. If none qualifies, fail clearly rather than substituting an unverified headline or implying that news caused the price move.

For broad financial topic menus, rank by freshness and editorial relevance from direct publisher feeds. Call the list an editorial selection, not a popularity ranking, unless actual audience metrics are available.

**Why:** Google News RSS states it is intended for personal, non-commercial feed-reader use; the format needs a direct, verifiable publisher source, and news is only possible context.

**How to apply:** When changing finance news research, keep source domains, publication dates, and article links visible. Preserve asset-match checks for coin Shorts and treat thin or stale coverage as an explicit no-news result.
