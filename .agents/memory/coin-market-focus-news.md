---
name: Financial news sourcing
description: Publisher-source and evidence rules for finance market-focus Shorts and long-form topic research.
---

Use direct publisher or official-project feeds for the headline. Require a recent, coin-relevant item whose HTTPS article URL stays on the publisher's domain. If no item qualifies but at least one feed was checked, continue with a clearly labeled no-catalyst story using measured price, volume, and BTC/ETH comparisons; say the exact cause is unknown. Never invent a headline or claim that news caused the move. If every feed is unavailable, fail as an operational error rather than report that no news exists.

For broad financial topic menus, rank by freshness and editorial relevance from direct publisher feeds. Call the list an editorial selection, not a popularity ranking, unless actual audience metrics are available.

**Why:** Direct publisher evidence supports attribution, but absence of a qualifying headline should not block a factual market recap. Treating missing evidence as a confirmed cause would mislead viewers; treating failed feeds as proof of no news would also be inaccurate.

**How to apply:** Keep source domains, publication dates, and article links visible when a headline is found. For thin or stale coverage, show an explicit unknown-catalyst card and limit explanatory context to verified price, volume, and benchmark data. Preserve asset-match checks for coin Shorts.
