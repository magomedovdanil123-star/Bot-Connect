---
name: Coin market-focus news
description: Publisher-source and relevance rules for the 24-hour coin market Shorts format.
---

Use direct publisher or official-project feeds for the headline. Require a recent, coin-relevant item whose HTTPS article URL stays on the publisher's domain. If none qualifies, fail clearly rather than substituting an unverified headline or implying that news caused the price move.

**Why:** Google News RSS states it is intended for personal, non-commercial feed-reader use; the format needs a direct, verifiable publisher source, and news is only possible context.

**How to apply:** When expanding or changing market-focus news sources, preserve source-domain, publication-time, and asset-match checks. Treat thin coverage as an explicit no-news result.
