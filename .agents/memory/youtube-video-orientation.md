---
name: YouTube video orientation
description: Channel variants must preserve the source video's left-right orientation.
---

YouTube channel variants must never apply horizontal mirroring; captions, interfaces, and people need to remain readable and correctly oriented.

**Why:** Mirrored output makes text unreadable and changes the apparent side of people and objects in the source recording.

**How to apply:** Keep channel styling limited to color, audio, greetings, and overlays; do not add hflip or other horizontal reflection filters.
