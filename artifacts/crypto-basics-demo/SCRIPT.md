# Crypto Basics — Motion Demo

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** The viewer understands cryptocurrency as a digital asset recorded in a network, sees where blockchain and access keys fit, and does not confuse the technology with a promise of profit.
- **Format and runtime:** 9:16 vertical, 28 seconds.
- **Identity:** No company branding. Charcoal studio field, restrained petrol green and mint, warm ivory type, and a small amber risk accent. Space Grotesk carries display type; DM Sans carries supporting copy.
- **Hero:** One oversized metallic token that transforms into a ledger record, a network of validating nodes, and finally a keyhole/ring. A small original presenter cutout appears only where a human explanation improves the read.
- **Motion:** Macro push-throughs, a traced transaction path, and split/aperture transitions. One primary event leads each beat; no market prices or invented data.
- **Arc and payoff:** Start with a direct question, move from asset to network to access, then resolve on a separate risk-focused end card. The token returns to its opening silhouette during the final handoff so the loop has a visual reason.
- **Sound:** One restrained instrumental electronic bed under calm Russian narration, with one short spoken line aligned to each scene. The music ducks during speech; no sound effects.

## Pre-code review

- Adjacent shots change composition and dominant motion, while the token/record/line/key/ring carry continuity.
- Every scene includes a subject transformation after the initial reveal. The 4b beat floor is met with a visible event at least every second, faster in the opener.
- Headline, captions, hero, and presenter have separate zones; supporting copy stays at readable short-edge sizes.
- No actual market data, speculative price direction, exchange branding, or guaranteed outcome is shown.
- The subject is educational and non-physical; no photoreal product kit is required. The only generated raster asset is an original transparent presenter cutout.
- All teaching points remain understandable with sound off; music is optional to comprehension.
