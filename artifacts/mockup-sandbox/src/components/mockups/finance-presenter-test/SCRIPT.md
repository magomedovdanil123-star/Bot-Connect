# Finance Presenter Test — Shot Script

Format: vertical 9:16, 18-second looping visual test, no controls inside the composition.
Topic: futures contracts. Voice track is intentionally omitted from this visual-only preview; captions demonstrate timing and karaoke highlighting.

## Shot 01 / 00.00–06.00 / Contract

- Purpose: Show that a futures contract agrees a price now for a transaction in the future.
- Exact text: kicker `ФЬЮЧЕРСЫ / 01`; hero phrase `ЦЕНА СЕЙЧАС`; caption `Цена фиксируется сегодня, а сделка будет позже.`
- Composition: Floating contract panel in the upper third, two counterparty tokens at its edges; illustrated host occupies the lower third, torso cropped by the lower frame.
- Choreography: Open on a fine gold orbit and a brief lens flare. Contract panel assembles from two glass slabs; a line draws between buyer and seller. The host arrives with an open-hand gesture, then makes a small nod. Caption words highlight in sequence.
- Handoff: The contract line stretches vertically into the collateral/position comparison.
- Sound: none in preview.

## Shot 02 / 06.00–12.00 / Leverage

- Purpose: Make the difference between deposit and exposure understandable at a glance.
- Exact text: kicker `ПЛЕЧО / 02`; hero phrase `ЗАЛОГ → ПОЗИЦИЯ`; caption `Небольшой залог управляет большой позицией.`
- Composition: Two luminous horizontal bars and a 10× scale marker above the host. Host shifts to a pointing pose on the right side.
- Choreography: The collateral bar fills first; a linked position bar expands to ten times its scale. The marker travels along the bar and gives one restrained pulse. The host points as the larger bar completes. Caption words highlight in sequence.
- Handoff: The moving marker accelerates into a payoff chart point.
- Sound: none in preview.

## Shot 03 / 12.00–18.00 / Outcome and risk

- Purpose: Show that price movement can create profit or loss and end on a clear caution.
- Exact text: kicker `РЕЗУЛЬТАТ / 03`; hero phrase `ПРИБЫЛЬ ИЛИ УБЫТОК`; caption `Движение работает в обе стороны — риск тоже выше.`
- Composition: A two-sided payoff graph occupies the upper half. A moving price marker crosses the entry line. Host changes to a thoughtful, then cautionary expression.
- Choreography: The positive and negative curves draw from the entry point in contrasting champagne and coral. A marker travels across the graph; each side lights only as it is reached. The host moves from thoughtful hand-to-chin to a concise open-palm warning. Caption words highlight in sequence.
- Handoff: The marker traces a thin gold arc that returns to the opening orbit without a blank frame.
- Sound: none in preview.
