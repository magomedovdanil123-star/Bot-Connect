# Pump.fun fees

- **URL:** https://pump.fun/docs/fees
- **Publisher:** Pump.fun
- **Type:** Primary fee documentation
- **Last updated on page:** 2026-05-20

The page lists a 1.25% total fee on bonding-curve trades, split among creator and protocol fees under the displayed schedule. Canonical PumpSwap pool fees vary by market-cap band; non-canonical pools use another schedule. The page says applicable fees may change.

**Use:** distinguish the bonding-curve rate from variable PumpSwap rates.  
**Limit:** these are first-party terms, not a measured fee-revenue series.
