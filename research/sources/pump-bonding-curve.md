# The Pump.fun bonding curve

- **URL:** https://pump.fun/docs/bonding-curve
- **Publisher:** Pump.fun
- **Type:** Primary product documentation
- **Accessed:** 2026-09-30

Pump.fun describes its curve as a constant-product automated market maker using virtual SOL and token reserves. Buys raise the quoted price, sells lower it, and price impact depends on trade size. The project says coins reaching the graduation threshold automatically migrate to PumpSwap.

**Use:** explain product mechanics as Pump.fun documents them. This does not establish market share or future adoption.
