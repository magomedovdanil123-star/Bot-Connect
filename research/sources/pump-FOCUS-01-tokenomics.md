## Notable Claims Requiring Cross-Reference

- **“Community allocation” / airdrop:** verify the exact definition (users, creators, liquidity, or other recipients), eligibility snapshot, claim terms, and whether unclaimed tokens return to a treasury or are burned. Compare the official announcement/token terms with the token allocation table and on-chain distribution. **URLs:** https://pump.fun/ (publication date: not stated; as-of 2026-09-30; **tier 1**); https://tokenomist.ai/pump (publication date: continuously updated; as-of 2026-09-30; **tier 2**); https://solscan.io/ (publication date: continuously updated; as-of 2026-09-30; **tier 2**).
- **“Team/investor tokens are locked”:** identify the lock contract, start date, cliff, linear release cadence, beneficiary wallets, and any transfer or acceleration exception. A chart without contract addresses is not enough to establish vesting. **URLs:** https://tokenomist.ai/pump (publication date: continuously updated; as-of 2026-09-30; **tier 2**); https://solscan.io/ (publication date: continuously updated; as-of 2026-09-30; **tier 2**).
- **“Fees fund buybacks or burns”:** independently trace fee wallets, conversion transactions, buyback purchases, burn destinations, and governing authorization. A discretionary statement is not a holder entitlement; a burn of some tokens does not prove recurring revenue-funded policy. **URLs:** https://pump.fun/terms (publication date: not stated; as-of 2026-09-30; **tier 1**); https://solscan.io/ (publication date: continuously updated; as-of 2026-09-30; **tier 2**); https://www.coindesk.com/ (publication date: article-specific; as-of 2026-09-30; **tier 2**, independent reporting).
- **“Staking/governance gives holders value”:** verify whether staking exists in deployed contracts, whether rewards are funded by inflation or revenue, who can propose/vote, quorum and delegation rules, and whether votes bind the issuer. A social-media poll or roadmap is not governance rights. **URLs:** https://pump.fun/terms (publication date: not stated; as-of 2026-09-30; **tier 1**); https://solscan.io/ (publication date: continuously updated; as-of 2026-09-30; **tier 2**).
- **“Token holders own part of Pump.fun or receive fees”:** ownership, dividends, redemption, and creditor/equity rights must be explicit in enforceable offering/terms documents. Token possession alone does not establish any of them. **URL:** https://pump.fun/terms (publication date: not stated; as-of 2026-09-30; **tier 1**).

## Source Quality Assessment

- **Tier 1 — official/primary:** Pump.fun site and terms are the right sources for project claims, disclaimers, token purpose, and legal language, but the page version and publication date must be captured. They cannot independently prove that a claimed buyback occurred.
- **Tier 2 — reputable data/on-chain/reporting:** CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap, Tokenomist, Solscan, and established reporting can corroborate supply, unlock observations, wallet flows, and reported launch facts. They are not substitutes for contractual documents; aggregators may revise labels or circulating-supply methodology.
- **Tier 3 — promotional/opinion:** project social posts, influencer threads, exchange marketing pages, and unverified dashboards should be used only as leads. None should support a numeric tokenomics claim without primary/on-chain corroboration.
- **Access limitation:** all five required searches and six attempted fetches failed with callback payment/error responses in this run. No numeric claim is therefore asserted here.

## Gaps & Unanswered Questions

- Exact maximum/total supply, circulating supply at launch and on 2026-09-30, mint authority, freeze authority, and contract address.
- Dated allocation table: community/airdrop, liquidity, treasury, team, investors, advisers, market makers, and any ecosystem allocation.
- Current vesting contract addresses, cliff/linear schedule, next unlock amount/date, beneficiary categories, and all changes since launch.
- Whether any buyback, burn, staking, or governance mechanism is deployed and observable on-chain; funding source and beneficiaries if so.
- Whether PUMP conveys equity, revenue share, dividends, redemption, governance, or any other legally enforceable holder right; and which terms disclaim those rights.
- Whether project fees are paid in PUMP, retained by the project, distributed to another token/class of holders, or merely cited as a non-binding future use.
- Independent reconciliation of every supply figure against the mint account, token-holder balances, vesting wallets, burn addresses, and exchange custody.
